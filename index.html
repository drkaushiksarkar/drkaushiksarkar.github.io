About

Senior AI leader.
Strategy, engineering, and P&L in one seat.

Over 15 years across health, climate, and development finance, I have sat in the room where consequential decisions are made and built the AI systems that make those decisions better.

At IMACS, I founded and led a global AI Centre of Excellence from zero: setting strategy, building and managing the team, and owning delivery end-to-end. The outcomes: a production 7B-parameter LLM, a 6-agent orchestration platform, a 58TB data lake across 17 countries. The result of executive accountability, not individual contribution.

In the same period I closed eight-figure funded programmes with NASA, The Global Fund, and Amazon. Technical roadmaps that became institutional partnerships.

What makes me rare: I can set the board-level AI strategy and architect the solution myself. No translation layer. No loss of depth at either end.
